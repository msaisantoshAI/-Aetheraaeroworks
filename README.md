## 🛰️ About the Company

**Aethera Aero Works** is a dynamic engineering start-up established with a clear vision to serve the **Defense, Aerospace, and Power Generation** sectors through advanced manufacturing, precision machining, and engineering capabilities.

Located at **Hardware Park, Hyderabad**, strategically close to Rajiv Gandhi International Airport, our facility provides excellent connectivity to major industrial, aerospace, and defense establishments. With a highly motivated and experienced engineering team and modern manufacturing infrastructure, Aethera Aero Works is being developed as a high-precision, quality-driven partner for critical applications.

---

## 🛠️ Advanced Machining Facilities & Infrastructure

- **CNC Vertical Turning Lathe (VTL)** – 3 Metre Class
- **CNC Horizontal Lathe** – ~1,500 mm Diameter × 5 Metre Length (with Precision Grinding Attachment)
- **5-Axis CNC Machining Centre** – ~650 mm Capacity
- **4-Axis & 3-Axis CNC Machining Centres** – Up to ~2 Metre X-Axis Travel
- **3D CMM Metrology Lab** – Precision Inspection & Volumetric GD&T Verification
- **Welding & Fabrication** – Inert-Gas Shielded TIG, MIG, and Argon Arc Welding Facilities
- **Heavy Material Handling** – 10-Ton Overhead Crane
- **Facility** – Approximately 15,000 Sq. Ft. Manufacturing Shop Floor

---

## 🏭 Core Domains Served

1. **Defense**: High-precision missile components, structural monolithics, actuator housings, and guidance system mechanical parts.
2. **Aerospace**: Complex 5-axis aero-structural components, engine brackets, landing gear sub-assemblies, and lightweight airframe elements.
3. **Power Generation**: Steam and gas turbine blades, high-pressure rotor shafts, labyrinth seals, and heavy vertical turned housings (up to 3m class).

## 🚀 Tech Stack & Design Architecture

- **Framework**: React 19 + TypeScript + Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS (Dark aesthetic, glassmorphism, responsive typography, modern contrast)
- **Animation**: Framer Motion (viewport animations, interactive cards, smooth preloader, seamless workflow)
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Deployment**: Automated GitHub Pages workflow (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`)
